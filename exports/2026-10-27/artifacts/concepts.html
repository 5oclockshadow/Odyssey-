Providers (OpenRouter, opencode, Ollama) and LLM libs (langchain, dspy…) overlap — langchain can itself route. core logic · the two open mechanisms
Hebbian — co-retrieved pairs strengthen each recall:
W[i,j] += η·(aᵢ·aⱼ) − λ·W[i,j]
bounded decay keeps it stable; hot links bias future recall.
Q-rerank — candidates reranked by learned value:
Q[s,a] += α·(r + γ·maxQ′ − Q[s,a])
reward r from downstream use; ε-greedy keeps exploring.
