Read before pricing anything

  • Pricing is moving off seats: a Pilot study has seat-based pricing falling from 21% to 15% of SaaS companies in a year while hybrid models rose from 27% to 41% [12]; ICONIQ puts hybrid at 48% as the primary model [16]. Vertical agents price at $199 to $999 a month base plus usage, or $2 to $15 per resolved outcome [14]. The Institute's agents should be priced the same way, with a base and a per-round or per-report meter.
  • Outcome pricing only holds when accuracy is high enough to run unsupervised; below that bar contracts revert to seats with caps [15]. Every offer above keeps the owner's approval step, so it starts as hybrid, not outcome.
  • Trading education is a red-flag market: guaranteed returns, lifestyle marketing and hidden losses mark programmes built to profit from students [9]. Row 02 sells a traced knowledge graph of a method and its sources, never signals or advice, and shows losses in the record.
  • Rows marked "verify" carry price ranges from general knowledge, not from this pass's searches; rows marked "built" already exist in the engine or Studio. Only rows with bracketed sources are anchored to the landscape searches.
