Institute blueprint · v2/Anima · Bridago/15 October 2026

An institute staffed by the very research it studies

Every finding the institute accepts is put to work: a seed block, a constraint, a compiled program, a choice of substrate. Each role is held by the best current result and lost when a better result, or a refutation, arrives. The staff is the research, and the research is tested by doing the job.

Hiring = accepting a finding Firing = refutation Companion to Plan v4

Hypotheses H0–H7 and sources [n] refer to Plan v4. Module names in code are files in apps/engine/. Nothing here has been measured.

01 · The premise

Anima, applied to the institute itself

Anima claims the cognition lives in the seeded graph and the DSPy layer, not the model. If that holds, an institute's capability is the set of findings in its graph. So the institute is organised that way: research results fill roles, and the institute can only do what it has shown.

Capability is bounded by evidence

A role with no finding behind it is held by a fixed rule or by the owner, and is marked as a vacancy. Vacancies set the research agenda.

Every round is an experiment

Each answer the institute gives is produced by named findings, so each answer is also a test of them. Use is evidence, logged per finding.

Outputs cite their staff

A round's trace records which findings acted. Anything the institute publishes or sells carries the research that produced it.

04 · From finding to staff

Seven stages, one owner click

1 · Question

Written with its falsifier.

2 · Experiment

A harness cell in the sandbox.

3 · Result

With the shuffled control beside it.

4 · Shadow

Runs next to the current holder on live rounds, without acting.

5 · Promotion

Owner approves. The finding takes the role.

6 · In role

Re-tested weekly on the frozen split.

7 · Retired

On refutation or replacement. Kept in the graph as history.

05 · Ramifications

When a wrong finding is a wrong employee

Technical

A wrong result in a role acts on every round until it is caught, so errors have a large blast radius. Staff that test other staff can agree with each other for the wrong reasons; the frozen split and shuffled control are the outside reference.

Epistemological

Findings are tested by being used, which is real evidence and biased evidence: they mostly meet the problems they are good at. A finding counts as established only after replication outside the institute.

Societal

Research that is staff can be shipped. If H0 holds, a seed and a small substrate on a CPU carry the institute's capability to any school, clinic or small firm, and equally to misuse. Agent-speed publishing is capped by the mindful gate.

06 · Monetisation

Clients hire the research

Because the staff is the research, every product is a set of promoted findings, packaged. The margin case rests on H0: if the substrate role can be held by a small model, each round costs CPU time, and what a client pays for is the seed.

If H0 is refuted

The substrate role stays with a large model, and the register says so. Research-as-a-service, Rex tools and Tmpltz stand; the licence becomes "seeding lifts small models by X". The register makes a clean negative result visible as a staffing decision, which is the institute working as designed.

07 · Go-to-market

Sell what has been promoted

  1. Now. Research-as-a-service on the applied goals; the Anima site shows the public register and the graph.
  2. After P3. If H0 and H4 read well, a small rung goes into shadow for the substrate role; Pro opens with shadow scores; first design-partner licences.
  3. After P5. If the small rung is promoted, publish the 30B-to-LSTM chart as a staffing change; launch the licence and CPU lines.
  4. Year two. Domain seeds become their own roles; outside replication offered as a paid membership.
