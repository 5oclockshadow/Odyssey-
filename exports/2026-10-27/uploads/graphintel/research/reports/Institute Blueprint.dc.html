02 · Workforce architecture

Roles are prompts on one brain, not separate systems

Every agent is the same Jarvis Signature reading and writing the same Rex graph. A role is a standing intake plus the tools it may call. Agents do not message each other; they write nodes and edges, and other roles pick them up on their next round. The graph is the bus, the memory and the audit trail.

Scout

Ingests feeds and papers into source and claim nodes. feeds.py

Theorist

Turns clusters of claims into questions with a falsifier attached. No question is written without one.

Experimenter

Turns a question into a harness cell, runs it in the sandbox, writes the row. sandbox.py

Critic

Attacks every result: runs the shuffled control, looks for leakage, checks the retriever did not do the reasoning. reflect.py

Publisher

Proposes batches for the public graph and drafts posts through the mindful gate. publishing.py · mindful.py

Maintainer (OPS)

Watches traces, opens incidents, proposes repairs and code upgrades as diffs. heal.py · upgrades.py

The human gate

One owner. Four things never happen without a click: publishing to the public graph, merging a code diff, spending above the monthly cap, and changing the frozen evaluation split. Everything else runs. SEXTANT is the one agent that speaks for the institute externally; OPS speaks only to the owner.

04 · Ramifications

An institute that optimises its own graph

Technical

The seed that agents improve is the seed that agents are measured with. Without a frozen split and an external seed, gains can be the graph learning its own test. Energy can be Goodharted: a graph can become consistent by deleting what disagrees.

Epistemological

The subject and the researcher are the same system. Findings about Bridago written by Bridago need provenance on every edge and an outside replication before they count. "Established" on the site should mean replicated outside the institute.

Societal

If H0 holds, capable reasoning runs on a CPU. That lowers cost for schools, clinics and small firms, and lowers it equally for misuse. Automated publishing at agent speed can flood channels; the mindful gate caps it.

05 · Monetisation

Six lines, one dependency

The margin case rests on H0. If a seeded small model holds quality, each round costs CPU time rather than large-model tokens, and the seed, not the model, is what a client pays for.

If H0 is refuted

Research-as-a-service, Rex tools and Tmpltz still stand; the licence and CPU lines become "seeding lifts small models by X" rather than "small matches large". A clean negative result, published, is itself a selling point for a research institute.

06 · Go-to-market

In step with the experiment

  1. Now. Research-as-a-service on the applied goals; the Anima site with public graph and Member sign-ups.
  2. After P3 (small grid). If H0 and H4 read well, open Pro with the shuffled control and early tables; first design-partner licences.
  3. After P5 (full grid). Publish the 30B-to-LSTM chart; launch the licence and CPU lines; Rex tools to design partners.
  4. Year two. Domain seeds as the catalogue of record; outside replication programme as a paid membership.
