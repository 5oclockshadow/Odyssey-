The idea

Agent and graph, one and the same.

Every chat turn, every research task, every contact message is one round through one Signature. The round reads a slice of the graph and emits the graph back, repaired. Nothing is stored beside the agent; the agent is the store.

A small mandatory core is seeded. The rest of the ontology and topology is decided by the model at runtime and can change at any round.

The engine

The company is a readable file.

One DSPy Signature, one typed hypergraph, one priority queue. Chat jumps the queue; research fills the rest; the graph re-examines its own weakest edges every minute. Under 1500 lines, 95% of them text.

institute.yamlmodel, mission, seed core, research topics, app rules
signature.pythe one Signature; output is a depth-two Round
round.pyslice → predict → merge; Hebbian weights; seed never removed
main.pythe care loop: queue, workers, stdin chat, research forever
serve.pythe site's door: POST /round, GET /graph
test_engine.pynine tests: seed, merge, decay, slice, queue order, schema

What could go wrong

Four risks, named.

The research reports carry the counter-evidence to our own thesis. We would rather you read it there than find it yourself.

RegulationSignals are regulated in most places. Framed as published research behind an exam; entity and jurisdiction decided before billing opens.
Model dependenceOne model string in one yaml line. Any LiteLLM-supported model swaps in; the graph persists across swaps.
Self-gradingAn agent that writes its own record can flatter it. Records are closed by rule, losers included, and the energy is measured against ground-truth structure.
The thesis is wrongFour runs on a small open model settle it either way. A negative result is still a paper.
