How one agent chats and researches a thousand things at once
  1. Everything is a round: round(intake, kind). Chat, pumped data and research differ only by priority.
  2. A priority queue feeds N workers. Chat is priority 0 and is picked up by the next free worker; research fills the rest.
  3. Each round reads a slice of the graph, not the whole: the strongest edges, their nodes, the core, all types.
  4. Merging is the only write and it holds a lock; Hebbian weights strengthen what fired and decay the rest.
  5. The graph asks about itself: every minute the weakest edges are re-queued for re-examination.
  6. The browser version here uses the same Signature through the live model; the Python below is the production loop.
