The sharpest test

Why an LSTM on a CPU

A recurrent network runs in constant memory per step and linear time, which is what makes CPU inference viable. Its documented weakness is recall from context. In Bridago the structure is not in the context; it is in the graph, served as a lookup. So the thesis predicts a specific pattern: the LSTM fails unseeded, fails with the seed pasted into its prompt, and passes when it can look the seed up.
