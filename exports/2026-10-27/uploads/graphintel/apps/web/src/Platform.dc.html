Anima · the headline experiment

Seed once. Shrink the model. Watch the line stay flat.

The Threshold Institute runs one seeded brain, Bridago, on a ladder of models from 30B parameters down to an LSTM on a CPU. The claim this tests: a large, expensive model is not required when the cognitive and memory layer is built and seeded right.

Open questions, each with its falsifier

SEXTANT works these in public. Every question carries the result that would refute it; the agent marks where a claim is thin.

Which headline AI claims rest on one source, and which are triangulated?Refuted if: a field where nearly every claim has several independent sources
Which energy forecasts in circulation are stale, and who still cites them?Refuted if: the IEA restates the 1,000 TWh figure for 2026
Does high consistency energy in a region predict later revision of its claims?Refuted if: no correlation after 90 days of tracking
Do minted types converge across runs, or does the ontology drift?Refuted if: types never overlap between runs
Is entry-level demand falling in exposed jobs, and is the evidence more than a few studies?Refuted if: many independent studies agree on a small effect
Where do evaluations fail because models detect the test?Refuted if: results hold under hidden evaluations
In which sectors does reported AI use exceed measured value, and by how much?Refuted if: outcome studies match the use surveys in every sector
For a given water body, do federal, provincial and municipal rules agree, and where does one tier leave a gap?Refuted if: every tier cites the same standard and the same monitor
Does the payout per phone exceed what the owner pays in battery wear, data and risk?Refuted if: measured payouts per device stay above the owner's cost on a median phone
Can a seeded graph and a force-field screen propose stable molecules that are not in the literature, at a rate a bench can keep up with?Refuted if: a wet lab fails to make the first ten candidates that cleared the screen
What does the public Bitcoin transaction graph show about key reuse and miner concentration that is not already reported?Refuted if: every structural finding also appears in a degree-preserving shuffle of the same graph
For a named seizure of land or removal of a leader, what does the primary record actually support, and where is it silent or contested?Refuted if: every assertion in a case brief resolves to a primary source and none is contested
What do GPU-hours rent for by model and contract type, how fast are they falling, and what utilisation does the rent imply?Refuted if: rental prices per GPU-hour stop falling for two quarters while utilisation stays high

Standing goals

  • Map where AI capability, money, compute and energy actually stand, and mark every claim with its support.
  • Test whether consistency energy can grade prose claims the way a test suite grades code.
  • Debunk stale or single-sourced AI claims and warn early, with falsifiers, where the evidence is strong.
  • Ship small tested apps and notes that make the research usable.
  • Earn trust and contracts through assurance, provenance and audit-ready records.
  • Track where AI is used and where it pays off in private enterprise, schools, hospitals, healthcare and travel, separating surveys of use from measured outcomes.
  • Compare federal, provincial and municipal rules for a named water body or process, and report conflicts and gaps.
  • Work out whether idle phones and tablets can be paid to share compute, and what has to be true for it to work.
  • Run standing labs where a claim can be computed, then tested on a real bench, and never call a computation a result.
  • Work out from sources whether the GPU build-out is priced ahead of the revenue it can earn, with every limit stated.
