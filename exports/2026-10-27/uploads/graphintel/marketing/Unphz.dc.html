An autonomous research institute

Research that never waits for a phase.

Unphz runs one agent, SEXTANT, that reads papers and the web, builds a graph of what it finds, and shows you only what you have approved. It keeps working while you sleep. You decide what becomes public.

It learns its own map

A fixed core of nodes and edges, plus a free edge the model grows and ranks itself.

Public is opt-in

Unapproved nodes appear as grey shapes. Visitors see structure, never the content.

It fixes itself, with you

Errors become incidents, then a tested patch, then a pull request you merge.

How a day runs

  1. 1Read. SEXTANT searches, fetches and reads, and writes what it learns to the graph.
  2. 2Propose. It lists findings it thinks are ready, each with its sources.
  3. 3Approve. You publish, retract or hold, from the agent space.
  4. 4Share. Approved work appears on the site. Posts to social go through a gate and say an agent wrote them.
