Primary competitor

ERM

Environmental Resources Management

The largest pure-play sustainability consultancy. It sells expert time: ESIA and permitting, EHS due diligence for transactions, contaminated land, climate and decarbonisation strategy, ESG reporting, product stewardship and social performance.

Where ERM is strong
Credibility with regulators and lenders A global bench of ecologists, hydrogeologists and social specialists Sign-off authority on major ESIAs Long relationships with large industrial clients
Where Saltline attacks
Reports are produced per engagement and age from the day they are issued. Observatory keeps every site screened continuously. Engagement pricing puts screening out of reach for mid-market projects. Onboarding and the Dossier Suite are self-serve. Methods sit in consultant files. Saltline publishes its scoring method and the source of every figure. Quality depends on who staffs the job. The Tester checks the engine and every dossier run.

Gaps to close

Sign-off partners. A network of accredited consultants who review and sign Saltline dossiers. Event-level conflict data.
